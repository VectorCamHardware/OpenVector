Pass / fail thresholds (starting values — tune against a known-good unit)
CheckLimitMeaning
Corners found (min %)Share of the board's inner corners detected. Low = board not fully in frame, out of focus, or glare.
Scale (min px/mm)Image pixels per real millimetre at the board centre.
Edge width (max µm)10–90% edge rise from a blurred-step fit, median of every black-square side. Lower is sharper.
Edge falloff (max ×)Edge width on the outer half of the board divided by the inner half. Detects field blur / tilt.
Distortion residual (max µm)RMS corner error against a flat-plane fit. Grows with lens distortion or a buckled board.
Lighting spread (max %)(brightest − dimmest) white level across the board, relative to the mean.
Contrast (min)(white − black) ÷ (white + black): black-square centres vs. the paper around the board. Printed toner on plain paper photographs at about 0.4, so this mainly catches flare or haze.
