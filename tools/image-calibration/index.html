Limit Details
CriterionLimitDefinition
Test Setup: corners detected≥ 80%Proportion of the 12 inner board corners that are detected. A low value indicates that the board is not fully within the frame, is out of focus, or is affected by glare.
Test Setup: tilt≤ 10%Change in image scale across the detected corners. A high value indicates that the board is not parallel to the camera.
Resolution≥ 50 px/mmImage pixels per millimetre at the centre of the board.
Sharpness≤ 80 µmMedian 10–90% edge rise distance, obtained from a blurred-step fit to every side of every black square. Lower values indicate a sharper image.
Distortion≤ 25 µmRoot-mean-square deviation of the detected corners from a flat-plane model. Increases with lens distortion or when the strip does not lie flat. Requires at least 6 detected corners.
Lighting: uniformity≤ 15%Difference between the brightest and dimmest white-paper regions surrounding the board, relative to the mean.
Lighting: contrast≥ 0.3(white − black) ÷ (white + black). Printed toner on plain paper typically yields approximately 0.4.
Lighting: clipped pixels< 1%Proportion of white-paper pixels at or near saturation.
