CheckLimitMeaning
Corners found (min %)Share of the board's inner corners detected. Low = board not fully in frame, out of focus, or glare.
Scale (min px/mm)Image pixels per real millimetre at the board centre.
Edge width (max µm)10–90% edge rise, median of all edges. Lower is sharper.
Edge falloff (max ×)Edge width at the frame edge divided by the centre. Detects field blur / tilt.
Distortion residual (max µm)RMS corner error against a flat-plane fit. Grows with lens distortion or a buckled board.
Lighting spread (max %)(brightest − dimmest) white level across the board, relative to the mean.
