VectorCam Build Guide

An open-source guide to building VectorCam — the most intelligent solution in malaria surveillance.

VectorCam's answer to malaria surveillance pairs an ordinary smartphone with a small magnifying lens, a simple circuit, and an AI-powered app to read a mosquito's species, sex, and abdomen status in seconds. This webpage serves as a one-stop platform, documenting everything required to build a compatible unit yourself: from sourcing parts through deploying it in the field.
